HOW IT WORKS

Three dimensions

Weight — highlights physical handling effort.

Sales — highlights movement / demand.

Price — highlights value exposure.

Each dimension is ranked independently. The profile, for example AAC, keeps the operational signals visible instead of collapsing everything into one score.
i
ABC is decision support, not a final slotting rule.

Use the classification together with location constraints, client rules, batch requirements, handling limits and warehouse reality.
